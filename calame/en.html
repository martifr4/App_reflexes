The problem

Your job is advising clients. Not chasing them.

Tasks that keep coming back

Answering the same client questions, chasing missing documents, writing near-identical letters: time that goes neither into advice nor into billable work.

AI can help, but where to start?

The tools exist. You still need to pick the right tasks, set them up for your firm and agree clear rules on what may go through AI.

Professional secrecy is not negotiable

A fiduciary firm can't hand client data to just any tool, in just any way. Setting it up has to be done with care.

Confidentiality

Professional secrecy, taken seriously

You are bound by professional secrecy. Calame works in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP / nLPD).

  • An agreement signed before any work. A confidentiality and data-processing agreement, signed before we start.
  • Clear rules. Together we decide what may go through AI, and what may not.
  • Anonymisation when needed. Sensitive data is anonymised before processing whenever necessary.
  • Swiss-hosted email. Email exchanges with Calame go through a Swiss provider.
