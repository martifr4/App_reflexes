Typical engagements

What an engagement looks like

Three examples of 2–4 week pilot engagements, in different sectors.

Fiduciary firm

Sorting client documents before bookkeeping

Situation
Clients send receipts, bank statements and payroll changes in every format. Staff lose time sorting them before real work begins.
What we set up
An assistant reads incoming attachments, identifies the client and document type, fills in the firm's existing checklist and flags missing items. Data stays in tools the firm approves. Staff check every entry.
For the team
Accountants start from organised files and spend their time on review and advice.

Property management

Drafting replies to tenant emails

Situation
Managers receive a steady flow of tenant emails about repairs, rent and notices. Many need similar, carefully worded answers.
What we set up
An Outlook assistant sorts each email by type and drafts a reply from the agency's approved templates. Tenant data stays within tools the agency approves. The manager edits and sends every reply.
For the team
Faster, consistent answers, and managers still approve every message that leaves the office.

Supplier to international organisations

Answering UN and NGO calls for tender

Situation
A small supplier wants to bid more often, but each tender means gathering the same company information again.
What we set up
A searchable library of past answers, certificates and references, plus an assistant that turns each tender into a requirements checklist and drafts sections. The team completes, checks and signs off every submission.
For the team
More bids submitted, fewer missed requirements, less rewriting from scratch.

Questions

AI consulting in Geneva: common questions

What is a Forward Deployed Engineer?

An engineer who works directly with your team, on your real tools and documents, rather than from a distance with general recommendations. The goal: a solution your team actually uses, not a report that sits in a drawer.

I need help with AI but don't know where to start. Can you help?

Yes. That's the most common situation. Email us one real task and we'll do it for free, so you can see what AI can do. Then a free 45-minute diagnostic call helps find where AI saves you the most time.

Which sectors do you work with?

We have experience with fiduciary firms: accounting, payroll and administration. Our method works for any Geneva business that handles emails, documents and clients: property managers, suppliers to international organisations, engineering firms, SMEs, associations.

How much does AI consulting cost?

CHF 30 per hour, and you only pay for the time actually spent. The first request and the diagnostic are free. We tell you the expected time before starting.

Do you work alone?

No. Calame IA is a team of three, based in Geneva and Zurich. We divide the work according to each task, so every request is handled by the person best suited to it. You keep one main point of contact: me.

Is my clients' data safe?

Yes. We sign a confidentiality and data-processing agreement before any work, we set clear rules on what may go through AI, and sensitive data is anonymised when needed, in line with Swiss data protection law (nFADP).

Which languages do you work in?

We work with you in French, English or Spanish, and AI can prepare letters and emails in French, English and German.
