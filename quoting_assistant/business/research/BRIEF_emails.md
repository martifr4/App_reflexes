# Brief: write first-contact emails (email stage)

You write the first email Martí Fernández-Real will send to each verified prospect, plus a one-line phone
opener. You do **not** send anything and you do not contact anyone.

Read `BRIEF_identify.md` (what we sell, to whom) first. The verified list gives each firm's processes,
quoting gaps and town: use them to personalise. Everything you claim about the firm must come from that list.

## Rules (from the founders, non-negotiable)
- Language: the firm's `language` column (CA = Catalan, ES = Spanish).
- Always address the company in the **formal plural**: Catalan "vostès" (verbs in 3rd person plural: "els
  podria interessar", "vostès decideixen"), Spanish "ustedes" ("les podría interesar"). Never "tu",
  "vosaltres", "vosotros", "vostè" or "usted".
- Don't write "com treballen avui" / "cómo trabajan hoy" for how they work: write "com treballen" / "cómo trabajan".
- No claims we can't back: no pilot counts, no client names, no statistics, no "x10". No invented facts about the firm.
- Short and to the point: 120–180 words in the body. Friendly, concrete, no hype, no exclamation marks.
- Every email ends with the opt-out P. S. exactly as in the examples.
- Every email includes our LinkedIn profiles (first contact), formatted as below.
- Careful spelling and accents. Catalan: "sol·licitud", "pressupost", "l'instant"; Spanish: "presupuesto", "rápido".

## Structure (adapt, don't copy word for word)
1. Who we are: "Em dic Martí Fernández-Real i, amb el meu soci Álvaro, ajudem tallers i subcontractistes
   industrials de Catalunya a respondre abans les peticions de pressupost." / "Me llamo Martí
   Fernández-Real y, con mi socio Álvaro, ayudamos a talleres y subcontratistas industriales de Cataluña a
   contestar antes las peticiones de presupuesto."
2. One or two sentences specific to the firm: something true from their website about what they do, and
   the quoting gap we saw (no upload, office hours only, generic form, no WhatsApp...).
3. What we do, in one paragraph: assistant on their website and WhatsApp, answers each customer at once at
   any hour, collects drawing, material, thickness and quantities, gives an indicative price with their own
   rates, passes requests to their team ranked by value. The final quote is always theirs.
4. Offer: free for a few weeks, no commitment; if it isn't useful we remove it and they pay nothing. We are
   nearby and can visit them in [their town].
5. Question: would it interest them? More information by email, phone or in person.
6. LinkedIn line, sign-off, signature, P. S.
