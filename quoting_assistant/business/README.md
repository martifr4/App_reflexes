# Business documents

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `prospects.csv` | Mirror of the prospect tracker (Claude Sheet: https://claude.ai/artifact/4EHf7bVJvkSKFgK3EJGyb1). Update `status`, `last_contact`, `linkedin_sent` and `notes` whenever we write or a prospect replies. `linkedin_sent`: `no`, or the date our LinkedIn links went out (include them in the next email if `no`). |
| `outreach_log.csv` | One row per touchpoint (email out, reply in, call, opt-out), with date and Gmail thread id. |
| `outreach.md` | Funnel and per-company status, generated by `python business/outreach_status.py`. |
| `research/` | Prospect research: briefs, raw candidates per region, verified lists, email drafts. |
| `pricing.yaml` → `pricing.md` | Our prices and cost assumptions; run `python business/pnl.py` to rebuild the P&L and break-even in `pricing.md`. |

## Prospect statuses (same as the tracker dropdown)

`To call` → `Called – consent given` → `Emailed` → `Trial running` → (won / `Not interested`).
Reserves are marked `Reserve – not contacted`.

## Contact rules (from CLAUDE.md, section 4)

- Phone the company's published switchboard first; email only after a yes (LSSI art. 21).
- Every email carries an opt-out line. Opt-outs are honoured at once: set status `Not interested`
  and write `OPT-OUT <date>` in `notes`.
- Only contact details the companies publish themselves. Log every call in `notes`.
- The 23 Nov 2026 batch went out before this rule was adopted. If anyone objects, apologise,
  record the opt-out and don't email them again.
