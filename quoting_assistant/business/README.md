## Prospect statuses (same as the tracker dropdown)

`To call` → `Called – consent given` → `Emailed` → `Trial running` → (won / `Not interested`).
Reserves are marked `Reserve – not contacted`.

## Contact rules (from CLAUDE.md, section 4)

- Phone the company's published switchboard first; email only after a yes (LSSI art. 21).
- Every email carries an opt-out line. Opt-outs are honoured at once: set status `Not interested`
  and write `OPT-OUT <date>` in `notes`.
- Only contact details the companies publish themselves. Log every call in `notes`.
- The 23 Nov 2026 batch went out before this rule was adopted. If anyone objects, apologise,
  record the opt-out and don't email them again.
