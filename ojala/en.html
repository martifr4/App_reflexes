How it works

You play, and you always save.

Three steps and you're in. The only thing that changes compared with a normal savings account is that on the first Monday of every month, something might come your way.

STEP 1

Choose how much to set aside each week

€10, 20, 30, 40, 50 or more. It moves automatically from your usual account. And whenever you want, it comes back.

STEP 2

Every €10 saved is one entry

We count your average balance over the month. The more you save and the longer you leave it, the more entries you build up.

STEP 3

First Monday of the month, the draw

911 prizes: one of €10,000, 10 of €500 and 900 of €20. If you win, you'll see it in the app before 10 in the morning.

Where does the money come from?

The interest pays the prizes. Not your pocket.

Your savings are never gambled. They are deposited at a bank, which invests them in Treasury bills and government bonds. What that money earns each year, added up across everyone, is the pot. So, for every €100 you save:

€2.00 to the prize pot, €0.25 for the bank, €0.25 for Ojalá. That's all we charge.

This already works somewhere else

The UK has been doing this for 70 years.

They're called Premium Bonds, they're issued by the UK Treasury through NS&I, and they're the country's best-loved savings product. The idea is exactly Ojalá's: your money stays with you and the interest turns into prizes. It has been running there since 1956, and it runs very well.

1956Born in November 1956. Since 1957 the numbers have been picked by ERNIE, a machine now on its fifth version that uses quantum technology. It takes 12 minutes to generate the month's draw.
23 millionBritons hold Premium Bonds. Roughly one in three adults in the country. It's as normal as having a current account.
£135bnsaved in Premium Bonds. More than £135 billion that still belongs to its owners, who can withdraw it whenever they like.
4.35 %is the prize fund rate from September 2026. It moves with rates too: NS&I revisits it whenever the Bank of England changes its own. And the prizes are tax-free.
6.5 millionprizes every month, worth over £497 million. Two of them are £1 million each. Every month, two people become millionaires by saving.
£1 to £50,000Every pound is a bond, and every bond is an entry. Maximum £50,000 per person. Each bond has a 1 in 21,000 chance of winning something each month.

So why do people love it so much?

Because nobody loses. Britons talk about it as something very much their own: the grandmother who gifts Premium Bonds when a grandchild is born, the family checking the results together on the first of the month, the little thrill of "maybe this month". And the money, meanwhile, is still there.

Safety

Your money isn't at Ojalá. It's at a bank.

We provide the app and the draw. The deposits are held by our partner bank, licensed by the Bank of Spain, in an account in your name.

Covered up to €100,000

By the Deposit Guarantee Fund, just like any Spanish current account. With a Spanish IBAN.

Take it out whenever you like

Withdrawals with no notice period and no penalty. Your balance goes down, your entries go down, and that's it. No small print.

Entering is free

It's not a lottery or a game of chance with a stake: you never pay to enter. It's a promotional draw notified to the DGOJ.

Questions

What everyone asks us.

What if I never win?

Then you'll have saved. It's the only "lottery" where the worst case is having more money than before. With €20 a week for a year you have €1,040 in the account, win or no win.

What happens to the pot if the ECB cuts rates?

The pot comes down with them, and we say so in the strip at the top the same day. Today, with the deposit facility at 2.50%, this month's pot is €33,000: every 25 basis points move about €3,300 and, with them, about 165 pedrea prizes. The €10,000 Gordo and the syndicate prizes don't move; what adjusts is how many people get paid something. And your savings never move at all, whichever way rates go.

Can you only win €10,000?

Our aim is for you to be able to win much more. With a bigger pool, more collective savings mean bigger Ojalá Gordos. If there are 100,000 people with €300 saved each, that's €10,000 every month. If there are 1,000,000 people with €600 saved each, it'll be €200,000 every month. Around 28 million Spaniards play the Christmas Lottery each year. The numbers are transparent.

Why don't you pay me interest instead of running draws?

Because €1,000 at a Spanish bank earns about €25 a year, and that changes nobody's life. Pooling everyone's yield, someone collects €10,000. We'd rather there was a real chance every month than a small certainty for everyone.

Where does the prize money come from?

From the yield everyone's savings generate at the partner bank and from the bank's own contribution: for them, we're a way to attract savings without paying for campaigns. Ojalá keeps a small share of that yield. Never your money.

How are prizes taxed?

Prizes carry a 19 % withholding tax that we apply before paying it into your account. What you see in the app is what you get.

Can I enter without saving?

Yes. The law requires a promotional draw to be free, and it is: the terms include a no-deposit route to enter. But the whole point is that the savings stay with you.
